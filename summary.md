## TL;DR
A non-IIT engineering student shares their journey from securing multiple FAANG internships to receiving placement offers from top companies like Goldman Sachs, Google, and Microsoft. By prioritizing data structures and algorithms, they managed to excel in their internship experiences despite juggling academic responsibilities. Their approach emphasizes the importance of building a strong routine, consistent practice, and seeking guidance from mentors. They also highlight the significance of choosing projects that showcase their skills and versatility, as well as building connections with their family and peers.

## TL;DR
This is the story of an entrepreneur who achieved success through unconventional means, including manual labor, avoiding premature optimization, and solving problems others shun. He found success with a startup called Shipped, which was acquired by Target for $550M, and later founded his own company, Link, which grew rapidly and expanded into various product lines, eventually pivoting to an infrastructure model. Along the way, he developed key traits of a successful CEO, such as a willingness to pivot and focus on long-term growth over short-term gains.

### Starting Link [3:42](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PxGSdjlSsQY&t=222s)

1. side hustle Sidekick = Instagram growth bot
2. QR + NFC physical card pivot during COVID

3. $5/mo Link Pro sub for digital customizations
4. bundled CRM integrations → Link for Teams

### Link Phone & Blue Text [7:59](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PxGSdjlSsQY&t=479s)

1. AI phone number → auto CRM notes
   1. usage 10× vs business card
   2. $149/yr vs $99/yr
2. customers demanded iMessage (blue) texts
   1. no public API existed
3. built Link Blue → 50% demo attendance lift
   1. price jumped to $1,048/yr
   2. doubled ARR in 8 mo
